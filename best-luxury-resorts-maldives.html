12 Best Luxury Resorts in the Maldives for an Exclusive Escape

By Solace Travel Design· November 2026· 13 min read

Twelve resorts that define what an exclusive Maldivian escape means in 2027, compared honestly on atoll, style, signature experience and the traveller each one is truly for.

The Maldives has more than 160 resorts, and perhaps forty of them can credibly claim the word luxury. The gap between the good ones and the right one is the difference between a beautiful holiday and a perfect fit. A resort can be superb and still be wrong for you: too social, too large, too far from a reef, too polished for barefoot moods or too rustic for a milestone celebration.

This guide narrows the field to twelve. They were chosen not by star ratings, which inflate easily, but by the things our travel designers actually weigh when composing a Maldivian journey: the integrity of the architecture, the quality of the lagoon and house reef, the privacy of the villas, the depth of the dining, and the intelligence of the service. Each entry links to the resort's official website, because a composed journey always begins with seeing the real thing.

Prices move with season and currency, so we use tiers rather than false precision: $ roughly under USD 1,500 per night, $$ USD 1,500 to 2,500, $$$ USD 2,500 to 4,000, $$$$ USD 4,000 and above, always per villa before transfers and meal plans.

§How these twelve were chosen

Our designers evaluate Maldivian resorts on five axes: the lagoon (colour, calm, access to the open ocean), the reef (what lives within a fin-kick of your villa), the architecture (whether the villas were designed or merely decorated), the privacy (villa density, sightlines, who else is at breakfast), and the service grammar (invisible versus theatrical). The twelve below score strongly on at least three, and each answers a different kind of escape.

§The icons

The three names that redefined what a Maldivian resort could be. If you have seen a photograph of the modern Maldives, it was probably taken at one of these.

01

Soneva Jani

The resort that invented the modern overwater fantasy

In a country of superlatives, Soneva Jani still manages to be the photograph. A handful of enormous overwater villas scattered across a lagoon so pale it looks retouched, each with a private pool, a retractable roof over the master bed for falling asleep under the stars, and a polished wooden slide that delivers you, champagne in hand, into water warm as breath. There is no shoe policy, no news policy, and no hurrying of any kind.

What separates Jani from its imitators is scale and sincerity. The villas are among the largest in the archipelago, the service is genuinely familial rather than rehearsed, and the brand's decades-long obsession with sustainability reads as substance rather than signage. It suits couples who want theatre with their solitude, and families who want wonder without schedule.

Atoll
Noonu, northern Maldives
Transfer
Seaplane, approx. 40 minutes
Composition
Water and island villas, among the largest in the country
  • Best for: honeymoons, design lovers, families with older children.
  • The one experience: a private cinema on the sandbank, barefoot, under the stars.
  • Tier: $$$$
02

Soneva Fushi

The original barefoot-luxury island, still the deepest

Before the Maldives was a byword for luxury, there was Soneva Fushi: a densely jungled island in the Baa Atoll UNESCO biosphere reserve where villas are built from driftwood and thatch, shoes disappear at the jetty, and the night sky is treated as an amenity. Three decades on, it remains the most complete expression of the brand, and arguably the most soulful resort in the country.

The island is large enough to get lost on, which is precisely the point. Sunken seating, outdoor bathrooms, treetop dining rooms serviced by visiting chefs, an observatory, a zipline that carries you to dinner. The house reef is serious, and Hanifaru Bay, where manta rays gather in season a short sail away, is one of the great wildlife encounters on earth.

Atoll
Baa, UNESCO biosphere reserve
Transfer
Seaplane, approx. 30 minutes
Composition
Seventy-one jungle and beach villas, one to nine bedrooms
  • Best for: nature lovers, wellness travellers, long stays of two weeks or more.
  • The one experience: snorkelling with mantas at Hanifaru Bay in season.
  • Tier: $$$
03

Cheval Blanc Randheli

The most polished resort in the Indian Ocean

If Soneva is the Maldives' soul, Cheval Blanc Randheli is its haute couture. Conceived by architect Jean-Michel Gathy with palatial proportions, gallery-worthy art and a private island of its own for exclusive use, Randheli has a strong claim to being the most refined resort in the country, and the one against which all others are quietly measured. Condé Nast Traveller's editors name it simply the best for luxury.

The villas are immense and unhurried, scented by Dior, with private infinity pools that run long against the lagoon. Service is choreographed to the half-step without ever feeling staged. The Guerlain spa occupies its own island, as does the tennis court where a certain Swiss champion practises. Nothing here is loud, because nothing needs to be.

Atoll
Noonu
Transfer
Seaplane, approx. 40 minutes
Composition
Grand water and beach villas, plus a private island residence
  • Best for: milestone celebrations, aesthetes, anyone for whom "best" is the brief.
  • The one experience: dinner on the private island, barefoot, with the whole atoll to yourselves.
  • Tier: $$$$

§The private islands

For travellers for whom even the finest resort is still a resort. These two properties are the closest the Maldives comes to owning your own island without actually having to.

04

Velaa Private Island

Forty-five villas, a golf academy, and a submarine

Velaa is the Maldives' answer to a private members' club that happens to float. Just forty-five villas, many with their own spa rooms, wine walls and beachfront so private the staff arrive by hidden paths. It is the resort where privacy is engineered rather than promised: low density, high walls of green, and a clientele that values discretion as much as the turquoise.

The eccentricities are the charm. A golf academy with a Troon-managed academy and the country's only proper short game, a submarine that takes guests below the reef, a snow room in the spa, and a cellar that treats Burgundy with reverence. Velaa suits travellers who want a resort that behaves like an estate, and who intend to stay a while.

Atoll
Noonu
Transfer
Seaplane, approx. 45 minutes
Composition
45 villas and residences
  • Best for: total privacy, golfers, multi-generational families.
  • The one experience: the private submarine dive over the reef at dusk.
  • Tier: $$$$
05

The Nautilus

Bohemian luxury, entirely on your own clock

The Nautilus is the smallest resort on this list and the most unconventional. Twenty-six houses and residences arranged around a circular walkway in Baa Atoll, styled not like a hotel but like the private home of a well-travelled aesthete: brass, mahogany, curated clutter, art you want to touch. The founding idea is radical in the Maldives: there are no fixed opening hours for anything, and no rules. Breakfast at noon, dinner at midnight, spa at three in the morning, all simply arranged.

This "anytime, anywhere" philosophy, delivered by a butler to every house, makes The Nautilus the choice for travellers who find even fine resorts faintly regimented. The whole island can be taken privately, and often is.

Atoll
Baa
Transfer
Seaplane, approx. 35 minutes
Composition
26 houses and residences
  • Best for: free spirits, artists, full-island buyouts.
  • The one experience: a zero-rules week, starting with breakfast on a deserted sandbank at whatever hour you wake.
  • Tier: $$$$

§The grand houses

The great hotel brands brought their full grammar to the atolls. These four offer polish, depth of dining and service systems that smaller islands cannot match.

06

Waldorf Astoria Maldives Ithaafushi

A private island estate thirty minutes from Malé

Reached by a thirty-minute yacht crossing from the airport, Waldorf Astoria Ithaafushi spreads across three islands joined by walkways, and at four kilometres end to end it is among the largest resorts in the country. That scale is the selling point: 122 villas, each with a private pool, and eleven restaurants, including treetop dining and a chef's counter by a Michelin-decorated kitchen.

It is the strong choice for travellers who want the certainty of a great house: deep wine cellars, a proper spa complex, activities for every generation, and service trained to a global standard. The resort also offers a private island within the resort, Ithaafushi Private Island, for groups that want the estate entirely to themselves.

Atoll
South Malé
Transfer
Luxury yacht, approx. 30 minutes, any hour
Composition
122 villas across three islands
  • Best for: first-time Maldives visitors, food-focused travellers, convenience without compromise.
  • The one experience: the treetop dinner at Terra, nine metres above the lagoon.

07

The St. Regis Maldives Vommuli Resort

The fashionable island, where design meets ritual

On an uninhabited natural island in Dhaalu Atoll, the St. Regis Vommuli pairs the brand's signature butler ritual with the most architecturally playful design in the Maldives: a bar shaped like a whale shark, a spa resembling a lobster, overwater villas conceived as manta rays. By day it is barefoot; by sunset it is quietly glamorous, with a clientele that treats the evening bar as an occasion.

The Iridium Spa, set over the water with glass floors, is among the best in the country, and the butler service earns its reputation most in the details: unpacking done before you notice, preferences recorded with precision. A strong choice for couples who want polish with personality.

08

The Ritz-Carlton Maldives, Fari Islands

Kerry Hill's masterwork on a lagoon of its own

Designed by the late Kerry Hill, the Ritz-Carlton Fari Islands is the most architecturally refined of the grand houses: a hundred circular villas of pale timber and stone arranged in perfect geometry on a man-made lagoon, with light that moves through the day like an instrument. The restraint is the luxury. Nothing shouts; everything hums.

Part of the Fari Islands cluster, it shares a marina with the Patina, giving unusual depth of dining and a lively social counterpoint for those who want it. Families are exceptionally well served by the Ritz Kids programme and the Jean-Michel Cousteau Ambassadors of the Environment marine programme, while couples tend to claim the sunset-facing overwater villas and disappear.

09

One&Only Reethi Rah

Twelve beaches, one legendary island

One&Only Reethi Rah has been the Maldives' benchmark of resort excellence for two decades, and it earns the position the old way: space. The island is enormous by Maldivian standards, with twelve separate beaches, many reachable only by bicycle, so that even at full occupancy it never feels full. Villas are generously scaled, villas' privacy is absolute, and the service culture is the deepest in the country.

Everything here is quietly superb rather than novel, which is precisely why a remarkable share of guests return annually, some for a month or more. It is the resort we suggest most often to first-time visitors who want the Maldives done flawlessly, and to anyone who believes the truest luxury is room to breathe.

Atoll
North Malé

§The quiet classics

Three resorts whose reputations were built on intimacy, reefs and unpretentious grace. They are where designers send travellers who want the Maldives without the theatre.

10

Gili Lankanfushi

Barefoot overwater living, twenty minutes from the airport

Gili Lankanfushi is the connoisseur's Maldives: an all-overwater resort of rustic-luxurious villas built from sustainable timber, set in a vast lagoon just twenty minutes by speedboat from Malé. There are no shoes, no televisions in the villas, and no pretence of any kind. The result is the most romantic simplicity in the country, beloved by honeymooners and by a striking proportion of repeat guests.

The resort's coral programme is a decade old and genuinely serious, and the Meera Spa is reached by a jetty of its own. Because the transfer is by speedboat, you can be in your villa an hour after landing, which makes Gili the perfect first or final chapter of a longer journey.

Atoll
North Malé
Transfer
Speedboat, approx. 20 minutes, any hour
Composition
45 overwater villas and residences
  • Best for: honeymooners, eco-minded travellers, short add-on stays.
  • The one experience: dinner delivered by boat to your villa deck, alone on the lagoon.
  • Tier: $$
11

COMO Cocoa Island

The wellness classic, styled like a fleet of dhonis

COMO Cocoa Island occupies a small island in a southern lagoon of South Malé Atoll, reached in thirty minutes by speedboat. Its villas are shaped like the traditional Maldivian dhoni boats, curved and white, some floating free over a house reef so healthy that turtles commute past the steps. The aesthetic is white-on-white serenity, and it has aged beautifully.

COMO's wellness pedigree, the Shambhala spa and resident Ayurvedic doctors, makes Cocoa Island the natural choice for travellers whose escape is recovery: burnout retreats, post-wedding decompression, a serious yoga week. The food is famously clean and excellent, and the mood is determinedly hushed.

South Malé

12

Conrad Maldives Rangali Island

Two islands, and the world's most famous underwater room

Conrad Rangali is where Maldivian luxury grew up: two islands linked by a footbridge, the larger given to families, the smaller reserved for adults seeking quiet, with a spa village, twelve restaurants and one of the finest wine programmes in the Indian Ocean. It remains the resort with the most famous underwater experience on earth.

Ithaa, the all-glass restaurant five metres below the surface, dining on reef life drifting past the acrylic, is a genuine once-in-a-decade experience and should be reserved at booking, not on arrival. Above the waterline, The Muraka residence sleeps guests in a suite that descends beneath the waves, with its own chef and butler. It is theatrical, yes, but the theatre is superbly executed.

Atoll

  • Best for: gourmands, families with grandparents, experience collectors.

§Questions, answered

Exclusivity takes several forms. Velaa Private Island, The Nautilus and Kudadoo offer the smallest scale, with 26 to 45 villas and full-island buyouts possible. Cheval Blanc Randheli is considered the most polished ultra-luxury resort, while Soneva Jani offers the most singular overwater architecture. The most exclusive resort is the one whose style matches yours.

For the resorts in this guide, entry-level villas typically begin around USD 1,500 to 2,500 per night in high season and rise past USD 5,000 for signature residences. Overwater pool villas at the grand houses often run USD 3,000 to 6,000. Half-board or full-board plans, seaplane transfers and festive-season surcharges sit on top.

For honeymoons, Cheval Blanc Randheli and Soneva Jani lead for pure romance, Gili Lankanfushi and COMO Cocoa Island for barefoot intimacy, and Velaa or The Nautilus for total privacy. The right choice depends on whether your idea of romance is polished grandeur or castaway seclusion.

In the central atolls, Soneva Fushi in the Baa Atoll UNESCO biosphere is exceptional, with manta rays seasonally visiting Hanifaru Bay nearby. Gili Lankanfushi, Conrad Rangali and the Fari Islands cluster also offer strong house reefs. Serious divers usually look south to Laamu or the atolls beyond.

The dry northeast monsoon from December to April brings the calmest seas and the most reliable weather, and commands the highest prices. May to November offers greener islands, dramatic skies, superb diving with manta aggregations in Baa Atoll, and rates that can fall by a third. For value with dignity, late November is a sweet spot.

Speedboat resorts in North and South Malé atolls, such as Gili Lankanfushi, COMO Cocoa Island and Waldorf Astoria Ithaafushi, allow arrivals at any hour. Seaplane resorts, which include most of this list, operate only in daylight, typically 6:00 to 16:00, and add 30 to 50 minutes of spectacular flying. Late arrivals should plan a Malé night or choose a speedboat resort.
