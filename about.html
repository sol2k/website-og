The Society

A club for the well-lived

Solace began with a simple discomfort: that extraordinary travel had become ordinary: packaged, queued, photographed and forgotten. We wanted to return it to what it once was: authored.

Philosophy

Journeys composed, never packaged

We are a small society of travel designers, fixers and storytellers who believe the most important room in any journey is the blank page it starts on. Before we speak of hotels or flights, we ask about the last thing that moved you.

From that conversation we compose: a private tea ceremony in Kyoto with a master who accepts no visitors, a vineyard in Tuscany that has never sold a bottle, a wellness retreat in the Maldives built around your own rhythm. The logistics are immaculate; they are simply invisible.

What We Do

Four pursuits, one blank page

Everything the Society does sits inside four crafts. Each journey draws on all of them, in whatever measure the traveller needs.

Composed travel

Every itinerary begins on a blank page. Route, rooms and rhythm are designed around one traveller and never repeated for another.

Wellness retreats

Rest treated as design: days paced around sleep, stillness and recovery, with programmes chosen for your constitution rather than a brochure.

Cultural access

Doors opened by relationships. Private guides, family kitchens, ateliers and collections that never appear in listings.

A concierge for members

One message resolves the rest, from the first idea of a journey to the final airport farewell.

How It All Began

An idea born of a feeling

Every society begins with a longing. Ours began with the desire to find people worth travelling the world with.

The idea of Solace took shape when our founder, Stuti Verma, returned to India after several years in the United States. Her travels took her from the coasts of Hawaii to the cliffs of Madeira, from the quiet coves of Mallorca to cities too numerous to name. Along the way she gathered more than destinations; she gathered a conviction, that a place reveals itself through the company you keep in it.

Because the memories that lasted were never only about where she had been. They were about long dinners that ran past midnight, spontaneous detours taken on a stranger's recommendation, and the rare ease of finding people who looked at the world the way she did. What she missed, she realised, was not another destination. It was that company: a circle of people who shared her curiosity, her ambitions, and her appetite for discovering the world properly.

“The places were never the point. The people were.”

And so Solace was born. Not another travel company, and not another networking club. A private society that gathers like-minded people around shared interests, meaningful experiences, and a common love of life.

Wellness escapes and cultural journeys, music festivals and sporting events, gatherings in extraordinary places: travel is the thread that draws the circle together. The connections made along the way are what make it rare. Our ambition is simple to say and difficult to achieve: a global circle of people who value exceptional experiences, genuine friendships, and the quiet pleasure of being among their own.

The Founding Character

Every founding story needs its own founding character. Meet Sol: a golden-white Persian of considerable dignity, four-legged inspiration, resident personality, and self-appointed chairman of the society. He remains entirely convinced that Solace was named in his honour. We have not had the heart to disagree.

In the end, life is not about the places you visit. It is about who you share them with.

What We Hold True

Five quiet convictions

Depth over checklist

One room, one table, one hour of real access outweighs ten sights photographed from a coach window.

Privacy is luxury

Private guides, after-hours entries, closed doors opened by relationships, not by queues.

Wellness is design

Rest is not an absence of activity but a composition of it: pacing, stillness and ritual built into each day.

Culture needs a key

The most memorable hours of any journey are spent with the people who make a place what it is.

Service without theatre

A concierge who resolves before you notice. Excellence that never asks to be looked at.

Journeys, chapters

We design for the story you will tell in ten years, not the post you will make tonight.

“Luxury is not thread count. It is the feeling that the world, for a moment, has been arranged around you.”
