# Marketing Playbook — Solace Society

> How the club grows: channels, campaigns, and the WhatsApp concierge funnel.

## The funnel

**Discover** (SEO, social, referral) → **Dream** (globe interaction, experience pages) → **Speak** (call / WhatsApp / form hand-off) → **Compose** (designer conversation) → **Journey** → **Belong** (membership)

The site deliberately has no self-serve checkout. The entire UX funnels to **one number: +91 96504 70279**. Measure success in concierge conversations, not pageviews.

## WhatsApp concierge (the engine)

- Every form submission and floating-button tap opens WhatsApp with a pre-written message — zero typing friction.
- Reply SLA: within a few hours, personally, never a bot template for first contact.
- Track entry points by message prefix (waitlist vs contact vs experience card) to learn which sections convert.
- Use WhatsApp Business catalog for 4–6 flagship journeys once volume justifies it.

## Channel plan

| Channel | Role | Cadence | Notes |
|---|---|---|---|
| Organic search | Primary discovery | — | Follow `docs/seo-strategy.md`; Journal is the growth lever |
| Instagram / Pinterest | Aspirational reach | 3–4 posts/week | Cinematic imagery, one line of brand copy, "Link in bio" → globe section |
| WhatsApp broadcast | Member nurture | 2/month | One composed journey story + one seasonal invitation. Never promotions blasts. |
| Referral | Highest-quality leads | always | Members inviting members; thank both with an upgrade, not a discount |
| Google Business Profile | Local/brand capture | keep current | Categories: Travel Agency, Travel Lounge; phone = concierge line |

## Content pillars (Journal, when launched)

1. **The Composition** — how a specific journey was built (client-anonymized): the ask, the blank page, the result.
2. **The Door** — one place of rare access per post (after-hours museum, family vineyard) and the relationship behind it.
3. **The Season** — when and why to go somewhere (targets long-tail seasonal queries).
4. **The Pause** — wellness notes: pacing, stillness, recovery travel.

## Campaign calendar (seasonal spine)

- **Jan–Mar:** Alps & winter wellness ("The Alpine Pause" push) + honeymoon season (India wedding cycle).
- **Apr–Jun:** Japan (cherry blossom aftermath, before rains), Europe pre-summer.
- **Jul–Sep:** Maldives/Bali monsoon-value storytelling, Patagonia winter-spring expedition window.
- **Oct–Dec:** festive gifting — "the gift of a chapter" membership invitations.

## Brand rules for marketing

All public copy follows `docs/brand-language.md`. Especially: no discounts (offer access, not price cuts), no countdown timers, no exclamation marks. Scarcity is real (membership is limited) — state it plainly, once.

## Measurement

- North star: **concierge conversations started / month**.
- Supporting: waitlist submissions, membership enquiries, organic sessions on `/experiences.html`, returning-member journeys.
- Review monthly against this playbook; retire any channel that produces conversations at under ~5/month for two consecutive months.
